## 1. What Devonel is

Devonel (devonel.com) is the interim brand for a two-founder AI and software studio run by Sanchay Thalnerkar (Mumbai) and Umayr Sheik (Dubai), with Noora on the commercial side.
Sanchay's own framing: "we need to set up a proper a proper system and agency and team, avengers of sortd" (08-18) and "we need to make work speak" (09-02).
His rule for the whole thing is "1 skill 1 work 1 proof" (08-02) and "build a social proof layer that i am proud to look at from third person pov" (08-07).
Umayr's positioning line to the watch client: "I've positioned it more as a growth partner than an agency" (08-25).
Umayr's strategy statement (09-02 00:44) is the closest thing to a mission: "we build the face I talked about then start doing showcases for each capability, industry wide, use case wide. Something drops, we see whatever on twitter, get ideas, we have a rapid lab setup for prototyping and a pipeline to publish and we attack the SME space".
The operating model he wants: "if we keep converting the work into flows as we do it/discover methods/techniques we solid" (08-18) and "our goal is to cleanup, plant ourselves/our people/our tools and [move] to the next" (09-02).
The pain they sell against, in Sanchay's words: "we become the solution then" (08-13, replying to Umayr's "different vendors, no structure" at a bank) and "all these bots are broken, easy retainer w them" (08-14).
The name is provisional: on 08-26 Sanchay asked "should we go w devonel.com until we get a nice one?" and Umayr replied "Why not".
Alternatives were rejected: "insibl.ai" / "invisbl" ("nobody will ever remember its spelling", 08-25) and "fieldlab" (taken).

## 5. Target customers and how deals close

Geography: Dubai and UAE first (Omran, the watch boutique, tire retailers, DIFC network); India for build capacity and Sanchay's own clients; South Africa and Germany through Umayr's contacts; Australia through Devstrum.
Segment: SMEs and owner-operated brands with money and no technical bench; Umayr: "he is one of 1000 here" (09-02); sectors named: jewellery, watches, weddings, real estate (09-02).
Secondary segment: enterprises and agencies that "know what video generation is" but "dont know how to get good outputs" (09-02).
Sales motion is warm and relationship-led: every deal in the chats came from Umayr's network or Sanchay's; "we have like zero inbound leads in our life from content and yet here we are just 1 outbound impression and thats it we win them over" (09-02).
The gate is proof: "Nobody has anything to show up front to get the juices flowing" (Umayr, 09-02) and "people buy trust" (08-26).
Pricing models discussed: package-based costing plus customisations, which Umayr found "most common and easiest to begin with" after reviewing growth, digital and AI agencies (09-02); milestone or deliverable-based small first scope, then larger contracts (Noora, 09-02); hourly with "Min cost up front regardless of hours" (Umayr, 08-10); paid audit credited back against the build (Sanchay, 08-10); monthly maintenance retainer; commission or revenue share on sales.
Hard rule from experience: no unpaid multi-month starts ("thats a bad deal", 09-03).
Cost pass-through: model API and infra costs are billed separately or padded into the price ("2-400 usd padded will be easy to secure", 08-19).
Relationship over transaction: "if we want to actually build relationship etc we need to put in our own thoughts etc to make it actually good" (Umayr, 08-11); "Let's get him this so we can get more work from him" (08-26).
